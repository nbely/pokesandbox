# Open Questions (High-Level)

Questions for the project owner. Answers shape the MVP backlog. Grouped by theme and roughly ordered by how much they change the plan. Record each answer in the "Answer" column (or as a follow-up issue) so the decision trail lives in the repo.

## Product & game feel

| # | Question | Why it matters | Default if unanswered | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | "Recreate RBY": **authentic Gen 1 mechanics** or **RBY content on modern mechanics** (Fairy, phys/spec split, abilities, held items)? | Decides which formulas and Showdown format MVP implements | Modern mechanics, Gen 1 content; "Classic Gen 1" preset later | **Decided (Oct 2026):** **Authentic Gen 1 (Classic)** for MVP: Gen 1 battle mechanics, original Gen 1 learnsets and move data, original Gen 1 sprites, and no modern mechanics. `RegionRules` is **piecemeal** from day one: each dimension (species stats, types, moves, learnsets, catch rates, EXP/catch/escape/payout formulas, sprites…) picks its generation independently. Only battle mechanics are holistic (one simulator generation). Gimmicks (Mega/Z/Dynamax/Tera) are separate toggles, not MVP. MVP implements Gen 1 for every dimension; other gens follow. See [18 § Decisions](concepts/18-rules-time-and-randomness.md#decisions-oct-2026-piecemeal-rules-gen-1-first). |
| Q2 | Where does adventure play happen: **ephemeral (private)**, **per-player threads**, or **public channels**? Server-configurable? | Rendering, spam, RP/social feel, rate limits | Ephemeral by default, public option later | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Server-configurable, **ephemeral by default** (the only mode in MVP). Per-player **threads** are the preferred long-term mode, plus a channel mode. Highlights channel = follow-up. Viewing other players happens on the webapp (Discord lookup commands later). Players can make their **profile and/or characters private**. See [03](concepts/03-engine-boundary-and-game-loop.md#where-adventure-play-is-shown-decided-oct-2026-q2) and [13](concepts/13-characters-and-pokemon-instances.md#privacy-decided-oct-2026). |
| Q3 | How are **wild encounters** triggered: "Explore" button, rolled on traversal, or visible spawns? | The core-loop feel | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Explore buttons per method (classic). A press always yields an outcome from a weighted **explore table**: wild battle, trainer battle, or hidden item. Post-MVP **asides** (one-time flavor scripts). **Traversal requirements are MVP:** one-time route costs (specific unavoidable trainers, N battles/explores on the first pass) and permanent minimum costs per pass, counted in explores so Repel still helps (Repel'd steps are safe steps). Compiled into attemptable exit gates. **B (encounters while travelling) is not built**; permanent costs cover it. Kanto Classic sprites default to Yellow (color). See [10 § Decisions](concepts/10-encounters.md#decisions-oct-2026-q3). |
| Q4 | How do **trainers engage**: on entering, on explore, on talk? Do undefeated trainers block route exits? | Pacing, difficulty | On explore, optional per trainer; no blocking | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Per-trainer engagement mode (`onExplore` default, `onEnter`, `onTalk`). All trainer battles in a location count toward its traversal costs, however engaged. Multiple `onEnter` trainers run back-to-back as a generated gauntlet (no heals; beaten trainers stay beaten). Explicit gauntlets (E4) are scripts via the Gauntlet template (`resetOnLoss`, `lockedUntilComplete`). After-defeat visibility/dialogue is customizable per trainer. Gym leader availability is customizable via a Gate on the NPC using the new `location.trainersDefeated` fact. See [11 § Decisions](concepts/11-npcs-trainers-and-battle-definitions.md#decisions-oct-2026-q4). |
| Q5 | Is travel **instant** between nodes, or does traversing a route cost steps/time? | Pacing, encounter design | Instant | **Decided (Oct 2026, follows from Q3):** Moving between locations is instant. Route friction comes from author-defined **traversal requirements** (one-time and permanent costs) paid via Explore, not from travel time. Encounters-while-travelling (B) is not built. |
| Q6 | **Day/night model**: real time in the server's timezone, accelerated, or none for MVP? | Encounters, events | Real time, server timezone | **Decided (Oct 2026):** No day/night for the Kanto MVP (RBY has none; encounter tables use `any`). The engine still gets an injected Clock, defaulting to real time in the server's timezone when Gen 2+ content needs it. |
| Q7 | Do you want a **visual region map** in MVP or text-only navigation? | Asset pipeline, UI | Text-only + optional static map image | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Text navigation + optional static region map image (`graphicSettings.mapImageLink`). Interactive map later (webapp). |
| Q8 | **Level-ups mid-battle** (authentic) or post-battle (simpler)? | Showdown patch scope | Post-battle | **Decided (Oct 2026):** **Mid-battle, authentic.** On level-up the stats (and max HP, with current HP raised by the gain) update immediately, and new-move prompts happen during the battle. **Evolution is evaluated post-battle by the engine**, never inside the sim. Requires the state-injection patch to support mid-battle level/stat/move updates. See [14](concepts/14-battle-system-and-showdown.md#decisions-oct-2026). |
| Q9 | Full party: **auto-send to PC** or **prompt to swap**? | UX | Rule toggle, default PC | **Decided (Oct 2026):** **Prompt to swap in MVP** (QoL). Implemented without breaking atomic effects: the Pokémon is placed in the PC immediately, then the player is offered a non-blocking "Swap into party?" follow-up. Same flow for catches and gifts. See [06](concepts/06-effects.md). |

## Pre-MVP Arena

| # | Question | Default | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q10 | Arena teams validated against **a region's dex/rules** (needs a region first) or **server-wide standard formats** too? | Region-scoped formats + one built-in "Kanto Classic" format so it works before any region exists | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Not region-scoped. Custom content (fakemon, moves, items) lives in a **server content library** reusable across regions, e.g. sequels. Arena formats are **server-scoped**: built-in canon formats work out of the box, and admins can define formats mixing canon and library content (a region's dex is just an optional pool source). See [12](concepts/12-content-catalog-species-moves-items.md#server-content-library-decided-oct-2026) and [14](concepts/14-battle-system-and-showdown.md#arena-formats-decided-oct-2026-server-scoped-not-region-scoped). |
| Q11 | Level normalization (50/100/as-built) and **clauses** (species, sleep, OHKO, evasion)? | Lv 50, Showdown standard clauses | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Built-in formats are flat Lv50 (auto-adjusted) with Showdown standard clauses (Species, Sleep, OHKO, Evasion, Endless Battle), team preview, and singles/doubles variants (e.g. National Dex Lv50 Singles, Kanto 151 Lv50). Server formats configure their own. |
| Q12 | Team building UX: **Showdown paste import** acceptable as the primary path, with a guided builder second? | Yes | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Sequenced. Arena launch = Showdown paste import/export + random legal team + per-Pokémon validation errors. Next = guided builder (essential once servers have custom Pokémon, which external teambuilders don't know), built on Components v2 + FlowCord submenu/pagination flows, reusing the region Pokédex builder's Pokémon search flow. Later = webapp builder. Teams are **user-owned across servers**, capped at 50. Public team sharing is a later idea. |
| Q13 | Turn **timer** length and timeout behavior (random move vs forfeit after N)? | 90s, default move, forfeit after 3 | **Decided (Oct 2026):** PvP 90s per decision. Timeout = auto default move, 3 consecutive timeouts = forfeit. Challenges expire after 5 min. PvAI has no timer. |
| Q14 | Player-vs-AI: AI picks from **pre-authored teams per tier**, or **random legal teams**? | Random legal teams per tier first | **Decided (Oct 2026):** At launch, random legal teams generated from the format (works for custom formats and library species; heuristic movesets), with difficulty via AI profile. Admin-authored AI team sets later. AI profiles are our own subclasses of Showdown's `BattlePlayer`/`RandomPlayerAI` + `@smogon/calc` (Showdown ships only a random AI). |

## Architecture direction (the big alternates)

| # | Question | Recommendation | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q15 | Adopt **draft → published releases** (admins edit freely; players play immutable versions)? | Yes ([01](concepts/01-content-vs-state-and-publishing.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Yes. Draft → validate → immutable `RegionRelease`. Release notes are opt-in per server with a configurable channel (models now, UI later). Play-the-draft preview is later, but designed for now. See [01 § Decisions](concepts/01-content-vs-state-and-publishing.md#decisions-oct-2026). |
| Q16 | Commit to a **step-based Script system** for triggers/NPCs/gifts/gauntlets instead of bespoke features? | Yes ([07](concepts/07-scripts-triggers-and-events.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Yes, as long as scripts are easy to build (templates first). People-here list (to be cemented later). MVP = plain paged dialogue, with a `presentation` field reserved for rich embeds/Components v2 later. **Principle: everything is editable in Discord**, including a script step editor; the webapp is additive. See [07 § Decisions](concepts/07-scripts-triggers-and-events.md#decisions-oct-2026). |
| Q17 | Replace bucketed requirements with a **Condition tree** (all/any/not + predicate registry)? | Yes ([05](concepts/05-requirements.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Yes, refined as facts + operators, scoped evaluation, Gate envelope, usage policies, and dependency tracking ([05 § stress test](concepts/05-requirements.md#is-this-the-right-pattern-stress-test-against-scripts-quests-and-every-gate)). Lock presentation is caller-owned (`hidden` default / `locked` / `attemptable` → `gateBlocked` trigger). HM rules are creator content, with builder templates and suggestions post-MVP. Text syntax later, very low priority. MVP fact list decided against RBY needs (see [05 § MVP scope](concepts/05-requirements.md#mvp-scope)). Blackout respawn is character state, not a fact. |
| Q18 | Introduce **`libs/engine`** (pure domain package, actions in, events out) before player features? | Yes ([03](concepts/03-engine-boundary-and-game-loop.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Option C. Gameplay rules live in `libs/engine` (actions in, events out). Admin authoring gets the same treatment in a sibling `libs/authoring` package so the Discord builder and a webapp builder share one set of commands, validation, and integrity rules (see [03 § Authoring](concepts/03-engine-boundary-and-game-loop.md#authoring-the-same-pattern-for-region-building)). Built inside the monorepo (no separate repo/package), and extra scaffolding time is accepted to get the foundation right. |
| Q19 | Reference canon content by **Showdown IDs** and authored content by **content keys** instead of ObjectIds? | Yes ([02](concepts/02-identity-and-references.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Canon by Showdown ID. Custom species namespaced by an opaque **publisher ID** (display name shown in UI). Library entries are *original* or *linked* (pinned base + local patch), which covers both canon overrides and editable imports with clean upgrades; one definition per species ID per region. Move/item/ability overrides come post-MVP with custom moves. Authored content uses **opaque region-scoped keys** separate from `_id` (copy-safe; never player-facing; names resolved in UI). Locked in after the template-copy worked example (02). User server list = derived Discord membership + stored favorites/layout. See [02](concepts/02-identity-and-references.md), [12 § Species identity](concepts/12-content-catalog-species-moves-items.md#species-identity-canon-custom-species-variants-overrides--sharing-proposed-oct-2026). |
| Q20 | **Rebase and slim the Showdown fork** (patch set + mod) before integrating? | Yes ([14](concepts/14-battle-system-and-showdown.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Rebase onto current upstream and apply the revised patch set: generalized state injection, external choice hook, caught/ran endings, isolated custom-data dexes evolved from `dynamic-dex-data`, clean-up. **Fallback** if the rebase is painful: start fresh from upstream and re-apply only the patches we keep, as new commits. Rename the repo to `pokesandbox-showdown` after the org move. Monthly scheduled upstream-sync PR. Consumed by the monorepo as a **versioned package** (not a submodule). See [14](concepts/14-battle-system-and-showdown.md#decisions-oct-2026). |
| Q21 | Split **engine-managed flags** from curated progressions? | Yes ([04](concepts/04-progression-and-flags.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Two layers: curated progressions + open flags (engine-reserved prefixes + inline author flags; builder flag dropdown; "promote to progression"). Server-wide state in `WorldState`. Staff flag tools not MVP. Milestones/progressions **can** be lost. Conditions evaluate current state (revocation removes access), and gates can opt into `latch: true` ("once cleared, always cleared") via a reserved `gate:<owner>:passed` flag. See [04](concepts/04-progression-and-flags.md), [05](concepts/05-requirements.md). |
| Q22 | Pokémon instances as their **own collection**? | Yes ([13](concepts/13-characters-and-pokemon-instances.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Yes. One `PokemonInstance` collection with owner + `storage` (party/box, later daycare/GTS/loan). `partyOrder` (≤ 6 IDs) on Character. Released Pokémon soft-deleted then purged after 30 days. Original trainer recorded from day one (Gen 1: traded Pokémon get ×1.5 EXP and badge-capped obedience). See [13](concepts/13-characters-and-pokemon-instances.md#decisions-oct-2026-q22). |
| Q33 | Stay on **MongoDB** (properly configured, behind repository interfaces), with Postgres re-evaluated at Phase 3 and SpacetimeDB as a spike-only watch item? | Yes ([20](concepts/20-database-and-realtime-platform.md)) | **Decided (Oct 2026):** stay on MongoDB. Live PvP stays in Discord for the foreseeable future; moving play fully to the webapp is ~1–2% likely, so SpacetimeDB is shelved. |

## Scope & ownership

| # | Question | Why | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q23 | Will regions ever be **shared across servers** (install a published region on many servers)? | Ownership model, discovery | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Model A. Every region is owned by one server, and sharing = publish as template → others import an independent copy (keys preserved; "pull template updates" post-MVP). No live-linked installs; a shared cross-server world is Pie in the Sky. Template visibility private/unlisted/public; non-removable attribution; library dependencies come along as linked imports. **User-published templates are not MVP.** Official built-in templates (Kanto Classic) are sourced as described in [16 § Official templates](concepts/16-authoring-validation-and-portability.md#official-templates-proposed-oct-2026) (proposed). |
| Q24 | Is the **webapp builder** in MVP scope (for scripts/graphs), or strictly Discord authoring? | Authoring capacity for a full Kanto | **Partially answered (Oct 2026):** Discord must be authoring-complete. The webapp builder is an additive client of the same commands. Its MVP scope is still open. |
| Q25 | Is a maintained **"Kanto Classic" template** an official deliverable? (It doubles as the MVP acceptance test.) | Testing, onboarding | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Yes. "Kanto Classic" is a core-team-maintained public template (importable by any server). It doubles as the MVP acceptance test and seed/test data. |
| Q26 | What does `baseGeneration` mean today, and is it safe to replace with `RegionRules`? | Model cleanup | **Decided (Oct 2026):** Replaced by `RegionRules` (mechanics gen, learnset gen, dex scope). The magic default `10` is removed. |
